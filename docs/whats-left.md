## Waiting on a decision from Lami

**Subcategories.** Her category page copy suggests subcategories that do not
match the collections that exist. Jewellery is the clearest case: she suggests
Mixed Jewellery Packs, Jewellery Starter Packs and Phone Accessories, where
the shop has Necklaces, Earrings, Bracelets, Rings, Jewellery Sets and
Jewellery Starter Box.

Three ways to resolve it, and each changes what products need tagging:

1. Rename the existing collections to her labels
2. Create hers alongside, leaving the current ones in place
3. Map hers onto the nearest existing ones and change nothing

Until this is settled the subcategory tiles keep showing the current
collections, which work but are not what she asked for.

## Worth doing this week

**Collaborator access.** Working through the owner's login is fine to get
unblocked, not fine as a permanent arrangement. A collaborator account is
free, does not use a staff seat, keeps changes attributable, and means nobody
is holding the client's credentials. Lami gets the code from
Settings > Users > Security.

**A full pass over the live site.** Every page opened and read on a phone and
on a desktop, checking links, empty sections, forms and the checkout. Offered
twice and not yet done, because the store has been moving underneath us all
