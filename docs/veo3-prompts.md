## The script alone

For reading along, re-recording, or subtitles.

| # | Words |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Most people who want to start reselling get stuck in the same place. Not on effort. On what to actually buy. |
| 2 | I am Lami. I run GBG Wholesale Hub. We help people in the UK find stock they can genuinely sell. |
| 3 | *(B-roll)* Not a thousand units of something you are guessing about. Quantities small enough to test. |
| 4 | Because the problem was never wanting it enough. It is that nobody tells you what is worth buying. |
| 5 | *(B-roll)* You watch people selling on Vinted, on TikTok Shop, on eBay, and from outside it looks obvious. |
| 6 | Then you try it. And you are sat on stock nobody wants, working out what you got wrong. |
| 7 | *(B-roll)* So here is what we do. We hold stock here in the UK, in quantities you can start small with. |
| 8 | Jewellery, bags, clothing, beauty, electronics, homeware, kids, drinkware. Starter boxes if choosing it all feels like too much. |
| 9 | *(B-roll)* And the part most suppliers skip. How to price it, photograph it, and put it where people are already buying. |
| 10 | I am not going to promise you profit. Nobody honest can. What you make depends on what you pay and how you sell it. |
| 11 | *(B-roll)* What I can give you is real stock, straight answers, and people doing the same thing to ask. |
| 12 | So before I send you anything, I would rather know where you actually are. |
| 13 | *(B-roll)* There is a short questionnaire below. Eleven questions. About two minutes. |
| 14 | Your budget, what you are drawn to, how soon you want to start. No pressure, and nothing to buy. |
| 15 | Fill it in and I will point you at what actually fits. And you are in the free community either way. |

The six B-roll lines are not spoken by anyone on screen. Record them as
voiceover in Lami's voice and lay them over the cutaways, or re-use the audio
from a talking take. The cutaway prompts are deliberately silent so there is
no competing dialogue.

## Order of work

1. Generate shots 1, 2, 10, 15 first — all from Image A. If Image A holds
   across those four, the rest will hold.
2. Three takes of each clip. Keep the one that matches the others, not the one
   that looks best alone.
3. Cut in order, no transitions, one colour grade across all fifteen.
